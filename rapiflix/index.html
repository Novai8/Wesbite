A NOTE FROM RAPIFLIX

Made for the
movie curious.

Rapiflix is an independent film discovery and playback project. The free library resolves compatible video files from Internet Archive items and plays them inside Rapiflix, with saved progress so you can continue where you left off.

Rapiflix does not insert player ad breaks. Source availability and rights still depend on the underlying item; dynamically discovered Archive uploads are only played in-site when their metadata declares a public-domain or Creative Commons status. This check is cautious, not a legal guarantee.
