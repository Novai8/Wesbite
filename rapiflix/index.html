A NOTE FROM RAPIFLIX

Made for the
movie curious.

Rapiflix is an independent experiment in film discovery. The initial collection focuses on older films available from the Internet Archive, with more discovery features planned.

Public availability does not automatically mean a film is free of copyright restrictions. We aim to identify source and licensing information, but viewers and operators should verify each title's rights for their location and use.
